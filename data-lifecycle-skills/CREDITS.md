# Credits

These skills were written for this pack. They adapt ideas and structure from the projects below, published under the MIT License unless noted. Thanks to their authors. The last two rows before superpowers are credited for ideas only; no text was taken from them.

| Project | Author | What was drawn on |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills) | Matt Pocock | The process and vocabulary in `improve-architecture`, including its interview step (rounds, recommended answers, facts versus decisions), adapted from `improve-codebase-architecture` and `codebase-design`. Vertical-slice planning in `plan`. |
| [addyosmani/agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills) | Addy Osmani | Spec-first structure and verification gates in `spec`, `plan`, and `review`. |
| [vaquarkhan/data-engineering-agent-skills](https://github.com/vaquarkhan/data-engineering-agent-skills) | vaquarkhan | Grain, contract, idempotency, backfill, and service-level emphasis in `spec`. |
| [DataWithBaraa/claude-code-data-engineering-skills](https://github.com/DataWithBaraa/claude-code-data-engineering-skills) | Data With Baraa | The shape of `profile`, `impact`, and `incident`, the reconciliation in `test-reconcile`, and the cost pass in `review`. |
| [Building a Codebase Cleanup Skill for Claude Code](https://jonesrussell.github.io/blog/building-codebase-cleanup-skill-claude-code/) | Russell Jones | The existence-first ordering, tiers, and warning signs in `cleanup`. |
| [cursor/plugins](https://github.com/cursor/plugins) `deslop` | Cursor | The idea of a generated-code residue check, used in `build` and `review`. |
| [obra/superpowers](https://github.com/obra/superpowers) | Jesse Vincent | The evidence-before-claims rule used in `plan`, `review`, and `incident`. |

The deep-module vocabulary originates with John Ousterhout (*A Philosophy of Software Design*) and the term "seam" with Michael Feathers (*Working Effectively with Legacy Code*).
